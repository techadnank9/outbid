About

Outbid is a leaderboard you buy your way onto. No ads to sell, no algorithm to game, no application to be accepted. Whoever pays the most sits at #1 until somebody pays more.

Why it works

Most places that promise attention hide how you get it. Rankings shift for reasons nobody explains, and "featured" usually means someone paid without saying so.

This is the same trade, made obvious. The price of every rank is printed next to it. You can see exactly what the spot above you costs before you spend anything, and everyone else can see what you paid for yours.

What you actually get

  • A row on the board with your name, description and icon, pulled from your own page.
  • A direct link to your site, and a running count of the clicks it has sent you.
  • A place in a category ranking, where a smaller bid can still lead something specific.
  • A spot in the trending panel if people are actually clicking you.
