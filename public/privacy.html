Privacy

%BRAND% collects as little as it can get away with. This page lists all of it — not a category summary, the actual fields.

If you only visit

  • A random identifier is stored in your browser so you are counted once rather than on every page load. It is a random string, tied to nothing about you.
  • Visits are counted with DataFast, which reports page views and referrers in aggregate.
  • Clicking a listing records that a click happened, so the listing can show its click count. Who clicked is not recorded — the count is the whole record.
  • No advertising trackers, no data brokers, nothing sold or shared.

If you pay for a listing

Stripe handles the payment. From it we keep, against your listing:

  • your email address and name, as given at checkout
  • the Stripe customer and payment identifiers, and a link to your receipt
  • the amount charged, the currency, and the billing country
  • the card's brand and last four digits

We never see or store your card number. Stripe does not give it to us, and holding it would put this service in scope for payment-card compliance it has no business being in. Brand and last four are kept only so a payment can be matched to a receipt when someone asks about one.

Stripe processes your payment under its own privacy policy.

What is public

Your listing is public: the name, description and icon taken from the page you submitted, its rank, the amount bid, and its click count. Your email is never shown publicly and is not part of the listing.

Where it lives

In a single database on a server we rent, not shared with anyone. Access is limited to the operator of this service. Payment records are kept while the service runs, so refunds and queries remain answerable.

Your choices

  • Ask for your listing to be removed, and it will be.
  • Ask what is held about you, and you will get the actual records.
  • Ask for your payment records to be deleted, and they will be, except where we must keep them for tax or accounting.
  • Clearing your browser storage removes the visitor identifier.

Message @iamadnank9 for any of these.
